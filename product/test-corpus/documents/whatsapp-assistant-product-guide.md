# WhatsApp Assistant Product Guide

Product: WhatsApp Assistant by 22nd X AI. This is a synthetic test document.
Guide version: 2.3.

## What the product does

WhatsApp Assistant answers customer questions on WhatsApp. It books appointments.
It sends difficult conversations to a human agent.

## Setup

1. Connect a verified WhatsApp Business number on the Channels page.
2. Upload your FAQ document on the Knowledge page.
3. Set your business hours on the Settings page.

## Limits

- One workspace can connect a maximum of 3 WhatsApp Business numbers.
- The assistant replies in English, Hindi and Tamil.
