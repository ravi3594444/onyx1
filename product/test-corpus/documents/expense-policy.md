# Expense and Travel Policy

Owner: Finance team, 22nd X AI. This is a synthetic test document.
Effective: 1 April 2026.

## Scope

This policy applies to all employees and contractors who travel for client work.

## Approvals

- Book all travel through the finance desk at least 7 days before departure.
- A trip that costs more than INR 75,000 needs written approval from a director.

## Allowances

- Domestic meal allowance: INR 1,500 per day.
- International meal allowance: USD 60 per day.
- Hotel limit for domestic travel: INR 6,000 per night.

## Claims

Submit each expense claim within 30 days of the trip. Attach itemised receipts.
Finance does not pay a claim that has no receipts.
